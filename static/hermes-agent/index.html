Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is a personal, single-user AI assistant that John You runs on his own server. It helps him with his own email, calendar and documents: summarising newsletters, preparing a daily briefing, checking facts, and keeping research notes.

Who can use it

Only John. This is not a product or a service, and it has no other users. The app's Google sign-in is used by one person, to connect his own Google account to his own software.

What it connects to

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts, always for John's own account. Sending an email, changing or deleting a calendar event, or modifying, sharing or deleting a file requires John's explicit confirmation first.
